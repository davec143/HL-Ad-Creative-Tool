Logo — the only approved artwork

Logo rules

  • Use the file, never a redraw. Never let an image generator draw the logo, and never retype “HITLIGHTS” in a typeface. In the Ad Builder the generator is told to leave the logo space empty, and the real file is placed onto the finished image afterwards. On flat-panel templates the reserved area is also wiped back to the background colour first, so anything the generator left there (a stray ®, a half-drawn wordmark) is removed.
  • Two colourways only: white on dark grounds and photography, black on light grounds. Both carry the ®. Never recolour it (no gold, no violet), never add effects, never stretch it — scale proportionally. The Ad Builder picks the colourway from the actual background behind the logo, and on a bright photograph softly darkens the photo (never the logo) so the white logo stays legible.
  • Brand field colour is exact. Image generators drift flat brand colours by a few shades per render. The Ad Builder snaps the violet field (Template 1) to #523875 and the cream panel (Template 4) to #FBF6EE on every size, so the three files match each other and the brand.
  • Clear space: keep at least the height of the square H mark free on all sides. Nothing overlaps the logo.
  • Size in ads: roughly 20–36% of the frame width depending on template and canvas; see each template's per-size note.
  • Placement: top-left for Templates 1, 3 and 4; top-centre inside the bubble for Template 2; top-centre for Template 5 square and portrait, top-right for its landscape.
